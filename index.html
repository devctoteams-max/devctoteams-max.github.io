Devcto Studio makes short educational videos about history and the stories behind today's trends, and publishes them as Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok videos under @devcto.teams.

The Devcto Studio app

Devcto Studio is a desktop publishing tool that runs on the creator's own computer. A creator logs in with TikTok, picks a finished video, reviews it on a posting screen (caption, who can view it, comments, duets, stitches and content disclosure), and the tool uploads it to their TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API. Nothing is posted until the creator presses Post.

What the app uses from TikTok

  • user.info.basic: the creator's display name and avatar, shown on the posting screen so they know which account they are posting to.
  • video.upload: sends a video to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft to finish in the TikTok app.
  • video.publish: posts a video to the creator's profile with the settings they chose on the posting screen.
