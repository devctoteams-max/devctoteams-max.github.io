About Devcto Studio

We are a small independent studio, based in Egypt, making short educational videos for a global, English-speaking audience.

Our mission

Most people meet history by accident: a news story, an anniversary, a question that goes viral. We use those moments to explain the history, science and origins behind them, accurately, in under a minute.

Editorial standards

  • Accuracy. Every factual claim is supported by at least two independent sources. Claims we cannot support are cut.
  • Respect. We do not cover tragedies as they happen, current politics or elections, rumors about real people, or anything involving minors. Settled history is told as education, without graphic violence.
  • Rights. We only use material we created, licensed stock footage, or public-domain and openly licensed archive art, with the license recorded and credit given where required. We do not use commercial music or film, TV or sports footage.
  • Transparency. Our narration is a synthetic voice and many illustrations are AI-generated. We label AI content on every platform.

Corrections

If you spot a mistake, email devcto.teams@gmail.com. We review every report and correct or remove videos that are wrong.
