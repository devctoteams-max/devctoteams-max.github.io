How a video is made

  1. Topic. We pick a subject people are searching for and find the history or science behind it.
  2. Research. The facts go into a brief, each with at least two sources.
  3. Script and voice. A short script, read by a synthetic narrator voice.
  4. Visuals. Public-domain archive art and photographs where they exist, licensed stock footage, and AI-generated illustrations where nothing else is available. Licenses are recorded for every picture.
  5. Review. Each video is checked for factual and historical accuracy, captions, sound and rights before it is published.
