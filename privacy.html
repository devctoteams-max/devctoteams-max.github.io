1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Devcto Studio ("Devcto Studio", "we", "us") handles personal information when you visit our website at https://devctoteams-max.github.io (the "Website"), use the Devcto Studio desktop app (the "App") with TikTok or YouTube, or contact us. Devcto Studio is an independent studio based in the Arab Republic of Egypt. For any privacy question or request, contact us at devcto.teams@gmail.com.

For the purposes of data-protection laws, Devcto Studio is the controller of personal information described in this policy, except where we say that the App processes data only on your own computer, in which case you remain in control of that data.

2. Summary

  • The Website has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising.
  • The App runs on your own computer. It has no Devcto Studio server. Your TikTok and Google login tokens stay on your computer and are sent only to TikTok or Google.
  • The App uses YouTube API Services to upload videos you choose to your own YouTube channel.
  • We never sell or rent personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • You can revoke the App's access to TikTok or Google at any time and ask us to delete anything we hold about you.

3. Information we collect

a) When you visit the Website

The Website is a static site hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). We do not use cookies, analytics or tracking tools. To deliver the pages and protect against abuse, GitHub automatically processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, in server logs. We do not have access to these logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

b) When you use the App with TikTok

When you choose Continue with TikTok, TikTok asks you to approve the App. TikTok then gives the App:

  • Access and refresh tokens that let the App act on your behalf within the permissions you approved.
  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar.
  • Posting settings returned by TikTok for your account: your nickname and username, the privacy options available to you, whether comments, duets and stitches are allowed, and the maximum video length.
  • Publishing results: the publish ID, processing status and, once public, the post ID of each video you post.

The App does not access your TikTok password, your other videos, followers, likes, messages, contacts, location or analytics.

c) When you use the App with YouTube (Google user data)

The App uses YouTube API Services. When you sign in with Google, Google asks you to approve two permissions, and the App then receives:

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens from Google, which let the App act on your behalf only within the permissions you approved.
  • Your YouTube channel's title and channel ID (permission youtube.readonly). The App reads these only to show you which channel a video will be uploaded to before you confirm.
  • Upload results (permission youtube.upload): the video ID and link YouTube returns after an upload you started.

The App sends to YouTube the video file you chose and the title, description, tags, category, privacy setting and disclosures (such as "made for kids" and "altered or synthetic content") you set for it. The App does not read your Google password, Gmail, contacts, other Google services, your other videos, comments, subscribers, analytics or watch history, and it does not use Google user data for advertising.

d) In both cases

The App uses the videos and captions you choose to post and the settings you select. It stores no information on any Devcto Studio server, because there is none.

e) When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, name (if included) and the content of your message. Our email is provided by Google (Gmail).

5. Google user data and YouTube API Services

  • The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App's YouTube features you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Devcto Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the App's upload feature you requested, is never sold, never used for advertising, never transferred to anyone except Google itself, and is never read by a human except with your explicit permission, for security purposes, or to comply with the law.
  • You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time on Google's security settings page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions (also reachable at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions).
  • When you revoke access, or ask us to, the App stops using your Google data and you can delete the stored token as described below. Devcto Studio holds no copy to delete.

6. Where information is stored

The App stores your TikTok and Google refresh tokens in a configuration file on your own computer (local device storage), and upload or publishing results in small text files next to each video, also on your computer. Access tokens are kept in memory only while the App is running. The App does not use browser cookies or similar technologies, and Devcto Studio does not receive copies of any of this data.

7. How information is shared

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. Information is shared only:

  • With TikTok, when the App calls TikTok's API on your instruction (to log in, read your posting settings, upload and post your video, and check its status). See the TikTok Privacy Policy.
  • With Google / YouTube, when the App calls the YouTube Data API on your instruction (to sign in, read your channel title and ID, and upload your video). See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • With service providers that host our Website (GitHub) and our email (Google), only as needed to provide those services.
  • When required by law, for example to comply with a valid legal request, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users, the public or Devcto Studio.
  • In a business transfer, if Devcto Studio is reorganized or transferred; this policy would continue to apply to existing information.

We do not share TikTok or Google user data with any other internal or external party.

8. How long we keep information

  • TikTok and Google tokens: on your computer until you log out of the App, delete them, or revoke access. TikTok refresh tokens expire after 365 days and Google refresh tokens expire if unused for 6 months or when you revoke access.
  • Profile, channel and posting settings: not stored; fetched each time the App needs them.
  • Upload and publishing results: on your computer until you delete them.
  • Emails: up to 24 months after our last exchange, unless a longer period is needed to resolve a dispute or meet a legal obligation.
  • Website logs: kept by GitHub under its own policy.

9. Security

The App communicates with TikTok and Google only over encrypted HTTPS connections and uses their recommended sign-in flows with PKCE, so your passwords are never shown to the App. Tokens are kept on your own computer and are never sent to us. Please protect your computer with a password and keep its software up to date. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will inform affected users and authorities of a breach where the law requires.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, including under Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 151 of 2020), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation and the California Consumer Privacy Act, you may have the right to:

  • know what personal information we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • delete your information;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • receive your information in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing;
  • not be discriminated against for exercising these rights; and
  • complain to a data-protection authority, such as Egypt's Personal Data Protection Center or the authority in your country.

To disconnect TikTok: log out of the App and remove Devcto Studio in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions.
To disconnect Google / YouTube: remove Devcto Studio on Google's security settings page.
To delete the data the App stored, delete the token lines from its configuration file and its result files from your computer.

To exercise any right, email devcto.teams@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity, and we will respond within 30 days.

11. International transfers

TikTok, GitHub and Google may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States and countries in Europe and Asia. These providers use legal safeguards such as standard contractual clauses for such transfers. By using the App or Website you understand that your information may be processed in these countries.

12. Children

The Website and App are not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us at devcto.teams@gmail.com and we will delete it.

13. Third-party links and services

The Website links to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and other sites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this one.

14. Changes to this policy
